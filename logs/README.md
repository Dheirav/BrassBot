# Game logs

Real games, pasted in with `tools/paste_log.py`.

    PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python tools/paste_log.py --name vs-bots-4p

Paste the Boomforge log panel, Ctrl-D, and it is saved here and parsed straight
away so you learn immediately whether it read cleanly.

These matter because every other strong-play reference in this repo is a quote
from a strategy guide or an agent's write-up, and two of those turned out to be
strategy-conditional claims being applied as universal law -- the yardstick's
"70-80 VP entering the Rail Era" is sourced from a line about "a heavy industry
player" and had been driving a diagnosis for months.

A log is evidence about what was actually played. And for checking the ENGINE,
the play does not even need to be good: any complete game tells us whether our
move generator offers everything a human legally did.
